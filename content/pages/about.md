      # About


      June Kuhn is an artist, musician, and researcher whose practice examines (in)human-technology relationships. Her work interrogates the mediums, formats, and attitudes we have in regards to the making with digital systems. She is currently a PhD student at Imperial College London in digital musical instrument design, using autobiographical design to explore the compatibilities of crip theory, trans studies, and feminist technoscience in DIY culture. 

      June is a regular performer, music curator, and exhibiting artist. As Trampbunny she live codes techno, pc music, and UK bass with the London Algorave community, and coordinated events internationally in UK, USA, and France. As softshadow she performs spoken word poetry and live-looping saxophone as ambient electronic soundscape, organising events in the London experimental music scene.  She has exhibited Press Conference (2024) at Hypha Studios, and Mercedonius (2022). She is a recipient of the Develop Your Creative Practice Grant in 2024, to develop nonsighted access to spatial audio content in the browser.
