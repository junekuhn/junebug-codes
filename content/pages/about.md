      # About

      June is a transdisciplinary developer and artist who uses code, sound, and pixels to make interactive, audiovisual experiences. They completed an MA in Computational Arts at Goldsmiths, University of London. Since 2018, June has exhibited work using sonification, spatial audio, websites, saxophone, and live code.
