      # About

      June is an artist, musician, and researcher based in London, UK.  She is currently a PhD student at the [Augmented Instruments Lab](https://instrumentslab.org), building a vocal instrument called Ephemerides and conducting research that integrates Crip Theory and Trans Studies into design. 

      June is active in the independent music scene in London, playing saxophone as softshadow and sometimes live code as trampbunny.
