2026 is fast approaching, one of the things I really feel the need to explore is erotic arts.

No, it’s not a euphemism for commerical porn, and it’s not just performance art that’s ‘sexy’. I think culturally we only have the space for the consumable fucking videos, the erotic novel, the hypno tape, strip clubs, and maybe the live performance art that contains nudity.

I think that erotic art is a cross-medium endeavor that bridges film and live performance in an interesting way. It’s been a “when I see it, I know it” phenomenon that transforms body motions into a kind of representation of “that’s what sex is to me”.

I think there’s a huge need for it as for most queer people, we learn about sex from watching straight porn and getting really confused, uncomfortable, or guilty. Where can someone learn about sexual acts that is more just words on paper, clickbait 5-minute videos, or fanfictions that depend on a perversion of an existing character (no shame to fanfic I love you guys!!).

I would like to express my body in a way that expresses its liberated potential. Moving beyond the basics of body-shame and dysphoria its flipside, reckless orgy and esctatic dance, there must be an outlet for doing erotics as an artform. It is not enough to capture the sex that is happening and reflect that unto an audience. It is not enough to ‘queer’ the queer porn, doing things that are unusual for the sake of doing things that are unusual. I’m … a designer. A musician. A programmer. I would like to express eroticisms in ways that not merely reflective of whatever oppression I’m dealing with but to truly show desire can be manifested.

And I’m not entirely sure what I mean by this yet.

One of questions that keeps popping up is, is erotic arts having sex in front of other people? Is erotic arts having sex with your audience in an indirect way? (audio porn seems to think so). Is that what I’m after? Having sex with my audience? As a musician it’s a terrifying concept because I do not fundamentally engage with a general audience with this in mind, and the people who watch my music would be appalled to think in terms of this. However, I think there is a right audience and that audience could emerge with the right intention setting.

The question of liveness in erotic arts is also an issue. The model I often see is the ‘bedroom producer’ of porn, who caters to a specific online audience, and feeds a certain kind of demand based on what they have to offer. This can be live streamed, but in most cases the person becomes a subscription product. And for many people, as demeaning as this might sound, this works. Mostly because, it’s a product, and it’s not a live, meaningful engagement. I don’t think the bedroom producers out there want that either, they’re giving the illusion of that in exchange for money. And I applaud them for figuring this out.

I have been discouraged, by most people I know (including sex workers), to pursue something that looks and acts like sex work, and I understand their thinking. To be the subject of what a public might think of me, is absolutely a daunting thought. And yes I do recognize it’s a privilege to approach this from a place where I don’t need it to survive. I hope I don’t lose sight of that.

inspirations
The first thing I think of tantra, which I know so little about. I know that it incorporates sexual energies into spiritual wellbeing, which I absolutely love as a concept. Here’s the thing though, I’m white, and it doesn’t feel right to pursue that specific direction unless I knew someone in my life who wanted to share it with me.

I’ve also yet to go to Anatomie Studio, which is a really cool intersection of kink, performance, workshop, and education. It fills all the boxes for me. But it seems expensive.

Of the erotic arts I know of, I’m interested in the live, meaningful engagement bit. There’s a group of East Londoners who run a party called Playbody, and I’m obsessed with them. I went to the party that this author went to, and it was absolutely fantastic as a day rave space. There are performances there that really bridge this gap between private sexual intimacy and the public event, and it’s not even a play party. But I left with that same glow, knowing I had participated in something so beautiful and intentional.

It was so obvious when I went this summer that this space was unlike most club experiences I’ve ever had, apart from a few music festivals. The objects are designed to be used and played with. But what’s even more insteresting to me is the erotic performance art night they had (but I didn’t attend) very recently. I think playbody is the thing I’m thinking of when as I describe erotic arts.

What’s the kind of event that facilitates this? I was hoping it was going to be Noughty Fingers, an event series I co-organised that aimed to bridge Algorave and queer raving. The realisation that I have with it is that algorave is much more about the ‘algo’ than it is the ‘rave’. That’s great, I have no problem with it, but yet again it’s not specifically what I’m looking for. There was a huge gap to make towards the kind of stuff I wanted to get away with. Noughty Fingers could very easily return, but I’d need to connect with a community that more easily fits the ideas that I’m trying to outline.

concluding thoughts
My friend Erin has a work of AI porn (very nsfw) that’s brought up a lot of interesting conversations between us. It’s on the complete opposite side of this spectrum, but it’s bringing up similar issues. AI porn creates the exact things that I was mentioning earlier that makes us so uncomfortable, there’s an erotic genre to traditional straight porn that probably has a name but I just don’t know it yet. It’s obviously many things that we would predict - the POV is always male, queer, BIPOC people are seen as ‘the kink’, etc.

There’s something to be said for reclaiming a medium that’s been so off limits your whole life by slapping your face on it. It’s a gorgeous instance of “yes, in fact, I can just do that”.

Well, what I’m interested in is obviously the medium (sex, role play, live performance, music, film, and sex tech) but finding different modes to express interactions with bodies through these.
Ideally, I think it would be fun to create a persona that could be expressed and enacted through all these mediums. And as far as what is paid, I think that depends on the level of intimacy of the experience. It could ALL be sex work, where intimacy is given through all mediums, but we obviously don’t treat a theatre seating like giving head.
