Artists, Educators, Researchers, or Computer Scientists who use screen readers or accessibility tools for the browser.

\- Open to any UK-based disabled artists or anyone who uses accessibility tools for the browser

\- 1-on-1 opportunity to help create better accessibility for virtual spaces using spatial audio

\- 1-3  paid £150 half day sessions at June’s studio in Deptford, London testing and providing feedback on browser-based spatial audio controls

\- Travel expenses included

\- Join a community of artists working with spatial audio and other immersive media on Discord

\- Experience with binaural audio preferred, but not required

If you have any access needs for this application please let me know via email or discord.
